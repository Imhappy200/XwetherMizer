# XwetherMizer
Made May 15, 2026
Have you ever wondered why your PC feels slower than it should? A lot of that comes from unnecessary bloatware and background services included with Windows. That’s why I made an app designed to remove up to 50% of that bloat and help improve your PC’s performance and your experience.

Features
Clears temporary files,
Disables Cortana,
Enables High Performance mode,
Removes Microsoft Edge,
And optimizes Windows for better speed and responsiveness
